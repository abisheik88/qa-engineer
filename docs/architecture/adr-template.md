# ADR-NNNN: Title stating the decision, not the topic

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision? State the facts, constraints, and forces at play — technical, ecosystem, and organizational. A reader should be able to see the problem without opening any other document.

## Decision

The decision, stated in full sentences and in the active voice ("Skills are authored...", "The release unit is..."). Include the rules a future contributor must follow for the decision to hold.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative that was seriously on the table, with the concrete reason it lost. Alternatives nobody would actually choose do not belong here.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what risks are accepted. List follow-up obligations the decision creates (checks to enforce, questions to validate, documents to update) — and where those obligations are tracked.
